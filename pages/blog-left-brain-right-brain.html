Left Brain vs Right Brain — The 90-Minute Secret Yogis Knew 5000 Years Ago That Modern Yoga Forgot

Your brain shifts every 90–120 minutes. Left brain = power to DO & ACHIEVE. Right brain = power to FEEL, REST & ENJOY.

left brain right brain yoganasal dominanceIda Pingalaultradian rhythm

Introduction — Why Your Yoga is Not Working Despite Doing Everything Right?

You do same asana, same teacher, same effort... One day body feels light, you fly in Vinyasa. Next day, same asana, you feel heavy, you get cramp, you get injured.

You sit for meditation... One day mind goes deep in 2 minutes. Next day, 30 minutes struggle, no peace.

Sister, Brother, it is not your body. It is not your mind. It is your BRAIN SHIFTING.

Your brain is not same for 24 hours. It shifts. Every 90–120 minutes, like tides in ocean. Ancient yogis called it IDA and PINGALA — Chandra and Surya Nadi. Modern science calls it Ultradian Rhythm.

1. The Two Gods Inside You — Left Brain and Right Brain

LEFT BRAIN — SURYA BRAIN — PINGALA NADI — Your DOER

Function: Logic, analysis, action, sequence, physical strength, decision, doing. The supplied article associates the right nostril flowing more with this state and calls it Surya Nadi ON.

RIGHT BRAIN — CHANDRA BRAIN — IDA NADI — Your FEELER

Function: Creativity, intuition, memory, feeling, relaxing, healing, enjoying, art, love. The supplied article associates the left nostril flowing more with this state and calls it Chandra Nadi ON.

A complete human needs BOTH, but the article proposes a shift every 90–120 minutes.

2. The 90–120 Minute Law

The article presents an ideal rhythm of Left ON for 90–120 min (Do) → Right ON for 90–120 min (Enjoy/Rest) → Left ON again, and discusses food, sleep, profession and body behaviours as factors that may disturb this rhythm.

3. Why Doing Hatha Yoga / Ashtanga Vinyasa in Right Brain Time Gives Injury

The article frames Hatha Yoga, Ashtanga Vinyasa, Power Yoga and strong physical asana as LEFT BRAIN activities, recommending practice when the right nostril is active. It gives a nostril test and describes possible outcomes of practicing strong asana when the left nostril is active.

The article also contrasts this with modern studios that teach the same powerful flow to everyone at the same time.

4. Why Meditation Fails When Left Brain is Active — And When To Sit

The article frames Meditation, Yoga Nidra, Pranayama, Mantra and Relaxation as RIGHT BRAIN activities, recommending practice when the left nostril is active.

Want to DO? Check Right Nostril ON. Want to FEEL, REST, ENJOY, HEAL, MEDITATE? Check Left Nostril ON.

5. Practical Sadhana — How to Watch, Check, Shift Every 90–120 Minutes

Step 1 — Check

Close one nostril at a time and compare which side is smoother and cooler. The supplied article uses the more open nostril as the indicator of which brain is ON.

Step 2 — Shift if stuck

For Left Brain / Do time, the article suggests Surya Bhedana, Kapalabhati and left-side sleeping. For Right Brain / Rest time, it suggests Chandra Bhedana, right-side sleeping and Sheetali breath.

Step 3 — Ideal Day Design for Yogi

  • Morning 5–6 AM — check; the article suggests meditation, Yoga Nidra and mantra.
  • 6–7:30 AM — after pranayama, strong Hatha / Ashtanga asana.
  • 9 AM–12 PM — work in Left Brain slots with 20-minute rest breaks.
  • 12–1 PM — lunch with attention to the nostril state.
  • 4–6 PM — light movement, creative work and music.
  • 9–10 PM — sleep and relaxation.

6. For Yoga Teachers — Check Nadi Before You Teach

The article asks teachers to have students check which nostril is ON before class and proposes stronger Hatha / Vinyasa when the right nostril is dominant, and softer Yin / Yoga Nidra / Pranayama when the left nostril is dominant.

Conclusion — Come Back to Rhythm

Start watching your nostrils. Start shifting every 90–120 minutes. Start doing asana when Surya is ON, start meditating when Chandra is ON.

Your brain has two wings — A bird cannot fly with one wing always ON. Learn to shift. Learn to live.
